Limited Assistance Representation (LAR)

Limited Assistance Representation (LAR) Services – Massachusetts Probate and Family Court

Limited Assistance Representation (LAR) is a flexible, cost-effective legal service option available in the Massachusetts Probate and Family Court that allows individuals to hire an attorney for specific parts of a divorce or child custody case, rather than full representation from start to finish.

In divorce and custody matters, LAR services may include advising on legal rights and obligations, drafting or reviewing court documents, preparing financial statements, parenting plans, and separation agreements, coaching clients for mediation or hearings, and representing a client at a single court event such as a motion hearing or pretrial conference. The attorney's role is clearly defined in a written agreement, and the client remains responsible for handling all other aspects of the case not covered by that agreement.

LAR is especially helpful for individuals who want professional legal guidance on complex or high-stakes issues—such as child custody, parenting time, child support, alimony, or division of marital assets—but who may not need or cannot afford full representation. It allows clients to maintain control over their case while still benefiting from experienced legal counsel at critical stages.

These services are authorized under Massachusetts court rules and are designed to improve access to justice by making legal assistance more affordable and tailored to each client's needs, while maintaining the same professional standards and confidentiality as full legal representation.

How LAR Works

With LAR, you and your attorney will create a written agreement that clearly outlines what specific legal tasks the attorney will handle. These can include:

  • Drafting or reviewing court documents
  • Preparing legal arguments for a specific hearing
  • Representation for a single court appearance
  • Providing legal advice for specific legal issues
  • Coaching for mediation sessions or settlement conferences

Benefits of LAR

LAR services can provide significant advantages for those navigating the Massachusetts Probate and Family Court system:

Cost-Effective

You only pay for the specific legal assistance you need, rather than a retainer for full representation.

Control Over Your Case

You remain directly involved in your case and make decisions about your legal strategy.

Targeted Expertise

You can access experienced legal advice for the most critical parts of your case.

Reduced Stress

Knowing you have professional legal assistance for key aspects of your case can significantly reduce anxiety.

Is LAR Right for You?

LAR is suitable for individuals who:

  1. Want to handle most aspects of their family law matter themselves but need professional help with specific legal tasks
  2. Have a relatively straightforward case but need guidance with complex legal issues
  3. Face high-stakes issues like child custody or substantial property division but cannot afford full representation
  4. Were already proceeding pro se but have discovered they need professional assistance with a particular aspect of their case
